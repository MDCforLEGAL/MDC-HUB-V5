# MDC HUB Legal

These pages describe how MDC HUB works at a high level. They are informational,
not legal advice.

## Privacy

MDC HUB uses Discord OAuth to create an account. When you sign in, the site reads
your Discord user id, username, display name, and avatar. It does **not** request
your email address, password, friend list, direct messages, or server list.

The site stores an opaque session token in your browser. The API stores only a
hash of that token, together with the profile information above, in its database.
The session lasts until it expires or you sign out.

Content you publish (scripts, comments, likes, and dislikes) is visible to other
visitors according to the section where it is posted. Comments hidden by a
moderator are excluded from public comment lists and public counts, but may
remain visible to administrators for moderation.

The site links to Discord and GitHub. Those services have their own privacy
policies. The site does not use advertising trackers or sell personal data.

- Understand that scripts and metadata from third-party catalogs belong to their

Administrators may hide or remove content, restrict features, or suspend accounts
that violate these terms. The site may change or be unavailable at any time.
