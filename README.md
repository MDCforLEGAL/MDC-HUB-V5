# MDC HUB V5

MDC HUB is a live Roblox script discovery and community publishing site. It
brings a searchable catalog, official MDC releases, member uploads, profiles,
comments, reactions, achievements, and a blue-check system into one interface.

**Live site:** <https://mdcforlegal.github.io/MDC-HUB-V5/>

**Discord:** <https://discord.gg/6ETYaxeSTU>

## What is on the site

- **Discover** — a searchable, filterable feed with game, category, status, and
  source filters.
- **ScriptBlox and Rscripts catalogs** — external catalogs shown alongside the
  MDC library, with source links and their original metadata.
- **MDC Scripts** — official releases published by authorized MDC accounts.
- **Community** — scripts shared by signed-in members, with likes, dislikes, and
  comments.
- **Accounts** — Discord sign-in, profiles, levels, XP, achievements, and blue
  check progress.
- **Comment moderation** — administrators can hide or restore comments; hidden
  comments are removed from public lists and counts.
- **Interface options** — Turkish/English language switching, a light or night
  theme, and a mobile or desktop layout choice on entry.

## Community rules

Keep uploads and comments respectful and relevant. Do not post content you do not
have permission to share, impersonate other people, spam, or publish malicious
payloads. Moderators may hide or remove content that breaks these rules.

Third-party scripts are shown for discovery. They are never executed by the site,
and every external listing links back to its original source.

## Privacy

Accounts use Discord OAuth. MDC HUB reads only the Discord profile information
needed to create and display an account; it does not request email, password,
friend list, or message access. See [LEGAL.md](LEGAL.md) for the full privacy,
terms, and disclaimer notes.
